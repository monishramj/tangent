Heapify fixes one broken spot in a heap.
A max heap keeps the biggest value at the root.
Here the root is three, but its child is nine. That breaks the rule.
So the parent swaps with its bigger child. Then we check again, one level down.
Sifting down costs big O of log n, because the tree has log n levels.
Building the whole heap takes big O of n, not n log n. That surprises most people.
The array is three, nine, seven, one, four. After one sift down it becomes nine, four, seven, one, three.
