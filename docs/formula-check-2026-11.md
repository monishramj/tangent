## Result

**30 detected formula regions, 30 requested, 30 decoded regions, 30 matched: 100% match rate**, above the plan's 90% threshold. Added pre-video waiting attributable to the lazy `enrich_formulas` RPC was **73.48 seconds**. Initial background ingest took **20.82 seconds**; the two RPCs together took **94.30 seconds**, with **95.35 seconds** total including monitoring/startup/cleanup. ## Input and method

- Owner's local `STAT 350 - Chapter 7 - Sampling Distributions.pdf` (102 pages), source pages **33, 39, 43, 58, 83**, copied in that order into a five-page temporary PDF. Chapter 8 was unnecessary.
- Selection heuristic: count `=`, `∑`, `σ`, `μ`, `√`, `±`, `≤`, `≥` in each PDFium text layer, choose the five highest counts (descending tuple order breaks ties), then confirm all five rendered pages visually. Scores were 33, 35, 36, 33, 44 respectively. This is a deliberately dense exercise/solution sample, not an unbiased chapter accuracy estimate.
- Real production JSON-lines RPC sequence: `ingest_pdf({path, assets_dir})`, then `enrich_formulas({path, ir, ids})` selecting every section ID of the five-page sample. No mock models, replacement algorithm, or synthetic IR.
- Scratch wrapper delegates unchanged to production `_convert_pdf` and `match_formulas`, recording returned formula boxes/text and actual wanted/found/matched maps; the production RPC loop, section selection and splicing still run. Assertions checked response IDs, pending-formula presence, and removal of the selected pending map. All 30 initial regions were empty-text pending formulas. Per source page, counts were **4, 5, 8, 6, 7**; decoded and matched counts were identical on every page. This measures regions Docling labels formula, not recall of every inline expression on the source.
- Match definition: nearest same-page bounding box with maximum normalized coordinate difference <= 0.02, plus nonempty decoded text. No fallback-to-original text counted as a match; there were zero unmatched selected regions. No claim of one-to-one matching on arbitrary documents.

## Separate follow-up proposed

No geometry/matching fix is indicated by this sample: the rate is 100%, so the below-90% contingency does not apply. The likely problems are recognition errors in visual tokens/digits and mixed prose/formula crops, followed by unconditional acceptance of any nonempty matched text; splicing wraps it in `$$` without checking alignment context or commands. Page 83 also shows overlapping/merged formula regions, so 30 matched regions should not be read as 30 unique clean equations.

Create a separate fidelity job: add small synthetic/openly licensed regression crops for missing digits, overbars, new/old subscripts, mixed prose, checkmarks and multiline equations; validate decoded LaTeX in its actual math context and flag source numeric discrepancies before accepting it. Decide the retry/fallback behavior there, then repeat a bounded real-page check and report transcription correctness separately from box matching. Do not silently trust source-layer fallback as repaired LaTeX.
